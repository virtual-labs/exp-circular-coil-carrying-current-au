## Assignment




<ol>
  <li>
    Consider a circular coil of <strong>radius 5 cm</strong> and having <strong>15 turns</strong>. 
    A current of <strong>0.5 A</strong> is passed through the coil. 
    Find the <strong>magnetic field at a point on the axial line</strong>, which is at a 
    distance <strong>10 cm</strong> from the center of the coil.
  </li>

  <li>
    For a circular coil of <strong>30 turns</strong> and <strong>diameter 10 cm</strong>, 
    find the <strong>magnetic field at the centre</strong> of the coil, if 
    <strong>1 A</strong> current flows through it. 
    Also, obtain the fields at different points on the axial line and 
    <strong>verify the Gaussian distribution</strong> of magnetic field.
  </li>

  <li>
    For a circular coil experiment, find the <strong>distance where the field due to the coil</strong> 
    is equal to the <strong>horizontal component of Earth’s magnetic field</strong>.
  </li>

  <li>
    Calculate the <strong>magnetic field at a distance of 10 cm</strong> from the center of a 
    circular coil of <strong>20 turns</strong> carrying current. 
    (Take <strong>radius of the coil as 5 cm</strong>).
  </li>

  <li>
    Find the <strong>dipole moment density</strong> of the given bar magnet 
    (<strong>length = 4 cm</strong>) using the circular coil apparatus.
  </li>
</ol>
